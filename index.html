Type of Materials Being Removed

Different projects produce different types of waste. Household cleanouts may include furniture, boxes, and general household items. Construction projects may involve materials such as drywall, wood, flooring, and other renovation debris.

The length of the project also affects dumpster selection. A short cleanup project may require a different rental arrangement compared to a long-term construction job. Planning ahead helps ensure the dumpster is available when needed and removed at the right time.
